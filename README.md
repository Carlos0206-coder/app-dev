# Stranger Things

> *"Friends don't lie."*

---

## Overview

**Stranger Things** is a popular American science fiction horror drama television series created by the **Duffer Brothers** for Netflix. Set in the 1980s in the fictional town of *Hawkins, Indiana*, the story begins with the sudden disappearance of a young boy named Will Byers and the appearance of a mysterious girl with psychokinetic abilities named Eleven.

---

## Key Characters

* **Eleven (El)** – A girl with powerful telepathic and psychokinetic abilities.
* **Mike Wheeler** – An intelligent and compassionate boy, leader of his friend group.
* **Dustin Henderson** – A smart, optimistic kid with a great sense of humor and love for science.
* **Lucas Sinclair** – A realistic, pragmatic member of the group who keeps everyone grounded.
* **Will Byers** – The quiet boy whose disappearance sets off the events of the series.
* **Jim Hopper** – The dedicated chief of the Hawkins Police Department.

---

## Key Themes & Elements

1. **The Upside Down** – An alternate dimension existing parallel to the human world, inhabited by dangerous creatures like the *Demogorgon* and the *Mind Flayer*.
2. **80s Nostalgia** – Features classic 1980s pop culture, synthwave soundtracks, retro video games, and vintage fashion.
3. **Friendship & Loyalty** – Highlights how strong bonds between friends help them overcome supernatural threats.
